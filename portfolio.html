Product architecture synthesis

把 brand code 設計成一套由入門、核心、升級到收藏的「可擴張系統」。

Design the brand code as an expandable system across entry, core, trade-up and collecting.

01 / Entry易理解的 precious 入口An understandable precious entry低選擇成本、容易送禮、保留品牌識別。Low choice friction, giftable, and recognisably branded.
02 / CoreDaily-wear iconDaily-wear icon建立穿戴頻率與品牌歸屬；以顏色、尺寸、材質拉闊深度。Builds wearing frequency and affinity through colour, size and material depth.
03 / Trade-up更高工藝與材質Higher craft & material以鑽石、寶石、可轉換性及複雜度提升客單與稀有度。Diamonds, stones, transformability and complexity lift value and rarity.
04 / CollectorHigh jewellery haloHigh-jewellery halo以非量產、故事與寶石資產建立權威，同時反哺全系列想像。Non-volume rarity, stories and stones establish authority and halo the whole system.
05 / Occasion場景化 clientelingOccasion-led clienteling生日、承諾、婚嫁、里程碑：產品結構必須對應關係節點。Birthdays, commitments, weddings, milestones: architecture must map to relationship moments.
06 / SKU discipline延伸不等於稀釋Extend, don’t dilute每一個新品應清楚回答：增加入口、提高轉換，還是提升 trade-up？Every new SKU should answer: extend entry, convert, or enable trade-up?
↗

產品策略結論：優先投資少數可被一眼辨識的核心 code，再以合理的價格梯、品類延伸和場景化 clienteling 放大終身價值；避免以無關連的新品數量製造「增長」幻覺。

Product strategy conclusion: invest first in a small number of instantly legible codes, then increase lifetime value through a rational price ladder, category extensions and occasion-led clienteling—not unrelated novelty volume.

03 / Tourism

第一方體驗入口First-experience gateway

旅客可建立跨國 clienteling 與購物轉換，但不能取代本地長期客戶經營。

Visitors create cross-market clienteling and conversion, but cannot replace domestic relationship building.

04 / Gold sensitivity

材料與價值心智Material & value mindset

以黃金同時作成本變數及消費心理指標；不同市場對重量、回收和品牌溢價的理解不同。

Gold is both a cost variable and a consumer-psychology signal; weight, resale and brand premium mean different things by market.

市場建議Recommendation

優先進入 Singapore，以 city-first、clienteling-first 的旗艦策略建立東南亞基準。

Prioritize Singapore first—using a city-first, clienteling-first flagship strategy to establish the Southeast Asia benchmark.

原因並非市場規模最大，而是它集合最高的購買力、區域財富與跨國客流；品牌可先驗證 assortment、服務與全價策略，再把可複製的 clienteling blueprint 擴展至泰國的旅遊／本地 affluent 雙引擎。馬來西亞適合精選城市，印尼則應在本地合作、價格透明與信任機制成熟後追求規模。

The reason is not sheer market size. Singapore concentrates the highest purchasing power, regional wealth and cross-border traffic. It lets a brand validate assortment, service and full-price architecture before replicating a clienteling blueprint into Thailand’s tourist / local-affluent dual engine. Malaysia merits selective cities, while Indonesia should pursue scale after local partnership, price transparency and trust mechanisms are proven.

Jewellery Pricing
& Margin Simulation

用一條 signature necklace 將零售價拆回單品貢獻，再以價格與需求變化，測試 margin 是否真的增厚。這正是 luxury merchandising 的基本語言：不是只有售價，而是每一個 SKU 的經濟性。

This signature-necklace model traces retail price back to unit contribution, then tests whether price and demand moves truly improve margin. It is the core language of luxury merchandising: not price alone, but the economics of every SKU.

假設控制台Assumption console

調整基準零售價、方案價格變動及需求變動。COGS 按每件固定，配送、零售及行銷按零售額比例計算。

Adjust base retail price, scenario price change and volume change. COGS is fixed per piece; distribution, retail and marketing are modelled as revenue-linked.
