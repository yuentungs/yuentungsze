規模不是唯一答案

Scale is not the only answer

市場規模可提示需求方向，但進入優先序仍須同時考慮財富密度、旅遊入口、黃金敏感度、客戶服務能力和產品價格梯。完整比較見 Case Studies。

Market scale signals demand direction, but entry priority must also account for wealth density, tourism gateways, gold sensitivity, clienteling capability and the product-price ladder. See Case Studies for the full comparison.
