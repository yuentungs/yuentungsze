📈 2026 市場趨勢總結

📈 2026 Market Summary

市場規模：受惠於印尼與越南中產階級崛起，東南亞珠寶市場規模在 2026 年已突破 136 億美元。
品牌價值：Cartier 憑藉其「藝術資產化」策略，轉售價值持續領先；Tiffany 則在數位原生代中佔據較高份額。
成本重組：AI 輔助設計與自動化排產使新品研發週期縮短 40%，大幅降低了品牌進入門檻。

Market Scale: Driven by the rise of the middle class in Indonesia and Vietnam, the SE Asia market has surpassed $13.6B in 2026.
Brand Value: Cartier leads in resale value through its "art-as-asset" strategy; Tiffany captures higher share among digital natives.
Cost Restructuring: AI-assisted design and automated scheduling have cut R&D cycles by 40%, lowering barriers to entry.

🔮 「一人公司」與電商邏輯

🔮 "One-Person Co" & E-commerce

去中心化生產：2026 年的「一人公司」利用 AI 智能體協調全球供應鏈，實現了極低固定成本的運作模式。
電商新生態：從「流量驅動」轉向「算力驅動」，AI 實現了千人千面的珠寶定製，重塑了電商的獲客邏輯。
可持續性：區塊鏈溯源已成為高端珠寶的標配，透明度成為品牌溢價的核心來源。

Decentralized Production: 2026 "One-Person Companies" use AI agents to coordinate global supply chains with minimal fixed costs.
New E-commerce: Shifting from "traffic-driven" to "compute-driven," AI enables hyper-personalized jewellery customization.
Sustainability: Blockchain traceability is now standard for luxury jewellery, with transparency driving brand premiums.
