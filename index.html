Jewellery Market Insights

透過 Google Trends 實時監測全球頂級珠寶品牌的搜尋熱度，從數據中提煉商業價值。

Real-time monitoring of global luxury jewellery brand search interest via Google Trends, distilling business value from data.

💡 如何閱讀數值： 圖表顯示的是「搜尋興趣比率」。數值 100 代表該關鍵字在特定時間內的搜尋熱度最高點；數值 50 代表熱度減半。這並非絕對搜尋次數，而是相對受歡迎程度。

💡 How to read values: The chart shows "Search Interest Index". A value of 100 is the peak popularity for the term; 50 means the term is half as popular. These are relative values, not absolute search volumes.

📈 市場總結 (Summary)

📈 Market Summary

卡地亞 (Cartier) 展現了最強的品牌基底，搜尋熱度長期領先，特別是在節日季（如 12 月）呈現爆發式增長。 蒂芙尼 (Tiffany & Co.) 的波動性較大，受聯名合作與行銷活動影響明顯。 寶格麗 (Bvlgari) 則在亞太地區展現出極高的品牌忠誠度與穩定增長。

Cartier demonstrates the strongest brand equity with consistent leadership, peaking during holiday seasons. Tiffany & Co. shows higher volatility influenced by collaborations. Bvlgari exhibits high brand loyalty and steady growth, particularly in the APAC region.

🔮 行業預測 (2026-2027)

🔮 Industry Forecast

1. 數位化體驗革命：AR 試戴與 AI 個性化推薦將成為提升線上轉化率的關鍵。
2. 可持續珠寶：消費者對「來源透明化」的需求將驅動品牌在供應鏈數據上的公開。
3. 品牌出海新路徑：中國品牌將透過數據驅動的精準行銷，在東南亞與中東市場尋求突破。

1. Digital Experience: AR try-ons and AI personalization will be key to online conversion.
2. Sustainability: Demand for transparency will drive supply chain data disclosure.
3. Global Expansion: Chinese brands will seek breakthroughs in SEA and Middle East markets via data-driven precision marketing.
