### English

AI pacing does not mean stopping AI. The harder question is who can afford to slow down first when every company and country fears that a rival may gain the advantage.

This article uses Simunovic et al.’s pre-emptive-strike experiment to explain how fear and limited visibility sustain the AI race. It then examines the market reaction, the likely winners and losers, and why the next phase is more likely to combine tighter oversight with continued U.S.-China competition than produce a global pause.
