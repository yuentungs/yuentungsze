當購買力受壓，消費者如何重新分配「值得花錢」的預算？

When Purchasing Power Tightens, How Do Consumers Reallocate What Is Worth Paying For?

從 UNIQLO、二手奢侈品、盲盒到旅遊：價值不只等於保值。真正改變的是，有限預算下每一筆消費都要回答「為何是它、為何是現在」。

From UNIQLO and luxury resale to blind boxes and travel: value is not synonymous with resale value. What has changed is that every purchase in a limited budget must answer “why this, and why now?”

核心觀點：性價比從來不是新現象。消費者一直希望以合理價格取得品質、功能與耐用性，UNIQLO 的 LifeWear 正是成熟例子。2026 年的不同在於，生活成本上升、可支配預算受壓、奢侈品經歷多輪加價，而二手平台、社群與 AI 比較工具同時降低了比較成本。消費者不是只問「買不買得起」，而是更頻繁地問：在旅行、盲盒、二手真品、珠寶與儲蓄之間，這一筆錢最值得投向哪裡？

Core argument: Value for money is not a new consumer discovery. Consumers have always sought quality, function, and durability at a reasonable price; UNIQLO’s LifeWear is a mature expression of that proposition. What is different in 2026 is the simultaneous pressure of living costs, constrained discretionary budgets, repeated luxury price increases, and lower comparison costs created by resale platforms, social content, and AI tools. Consumers are not only asking, “Can I afford it?” They are more often asking, “Among travel, blind boxes, authentic resale, jewellery, and savings, where is this money best allocated?”

引言：不是「買不起」或「不想買錯」的二選一

Introduction: It Is Not a Choice Between “Cannot Afford It” and “Do Not Want to Buy Wrong”

近年奢侈品市場常出現一句說法：「消費者不是買不起，而是不想買錯。」它準確捕捉了人們對價格、品質與品牌溢價的質疑，但若把這句話理解為購買能力已經不重要，便會錯過市場最基本的限制。

購買能力始終是價格判斷的底線。 當住屋、教育、醫療、保險與日常生活成本佔去更大比例的收入時，可用於非必要消費的預算自然收窄。一枚珠寶、一只手袋或一趟旅行不再只彼此競爭；它們同時與儲蓄、家庭支出、健康，以及延後消費的選項競爭。預算約束不會令所有人停止消費，卻會令更多人必須排序。

但預算也不是唯一解釋。一位客戶即使有能力支付，也可能拒絕某件新品，因為他認為設計、工藝、服務、稀缺性或二手流通性不足以支持標價。相反，一位預算有限的客戶仍可能為求婚、周年、家庭紀念或真正喜歡的作品作出取捨。因此，較準確的分析是：

購買能力決定「能不能買」；價值判斷決定「在可買的選項中，為何選擇這一件」。

這是 2026 年消費討論的核心。消費者並非全面走向低價，而是把有限預算重新配置到他們認為最能帶來長期使用、情緒回報、社交認同、人生記憶或未來選擇權的地方。

A common phrase in luxury discussions is: “Consumers are not unable to buy; they simply do not want to buy wrong.” It captures mounting questions about prices, quality, and brand premium. But treating the phrase as evidence that purchasing power no longer matters misses the market’s most basic constraint.

Purchasing power remains the floor beneath every price judgement. When housing, education, healthcare, insurance, and everyday costs absorb a larger share of income, discretionary budgets narrow. A piece of jewellery, a handbag, and a holiday do not merely compete with one another; they also compete with savings, family needs, health, and the option to defer spending. Budget pressure does not make everyone stop spending, but it makes more people rank their choices.

Budget is not the only explanation, however. A client who can afford a new item may still decline it because the design, craftsmanship, service, scarcity, or resale liquidity does not justify the price. Conversely, a client with a limited budget may still make a deliberate trade-off for an engagement, anniversary, family milestone, or a piece they truly value. The more accurate framing is:

Purchasing power determines “can I buy?”; value judgement determines “among what I can buy, why choose this?”

This is the heart of the 2026 discussion. Consumers are not moving uniformly towards the lowest price. They are reallocating finite budgets towards purchases that deliver longer-term use, emotional return, social recognition, life memories, or future optionality.

一、性價比不是新潮流：UNIQLO 早已提供另一種消費邏輯

1. Value for Money Is Not a New Trend: UNIQLO Has Long Offered Another Consumption Logic

將今天的現象稱為「性價比崛起」並不精確。消費者一直會比較價格、品質、耐用性與使用頻率；景氣較好時，這些比較未必被公開討論，但從未消失。UNIQLO 的發展說明，重視價值並非經濟下行才出現的防禦性行為。

Fast Retailing 將 UNIQLO 的 LifeWear 定義為：由採購、設計、生產至零售的整合，提供採用高品質、高功能物料，並以可負擔價格供應的產品。這套邏輯不以每週換款為核心，而是把簡約、功能、搭配彈性與重複穿著，轉化為日常衣櫥的長期價值。[1]

不過，這不表示 UNIQLO 已取代快時尚。快時尚滿足快速回應潮流、低入手門檻與新鮮感的需求；LifeWear 則提供基本款的穩定品質與較長使用週期。兩種模式可以並存，同一位消費者也可能同時購買兩者。值得注意的是，2026 年這種原本主要用於基本服裝的價值思維，正擴散至中高端時尚、奢侈品、珠寶與生活方式消費。

Calling the present moment a “rise of value for money” is imprecise. Consumers have always compared price, quality, durability, and frequency of use. In stronger economic periods, these comparisons may have been less visible, but they never disappeared. UNIQLO shows that valuing a purchase is not merely a defensive behaviour born in downturns.

Fast Retailing defines UNIQLO’s LifeWear proposition as an integrated model from procurement and design through production and retail, delivering products made with high-quality, highly functional materials at affordable prices. Its logic is not built around weekly newness; it turns simplicity, functionality, versatility, and repeat wear into long-term wardrobe value.[1]

That does not mean UNIQLO has replaced fast fashion. Fast fashion serves a need for rapid trend response, accessible entry prices, and novelty; LifeWear offers reliable basics and a longer use cycle. The two models can coexist, and one shopper may buy both. What matters in 2026 is that this value logic, once most visible in basic apparel, is spreading into premium fashion, luxury, jewellery, and lifestyle spending.

二、四股力量匯聚：「值不值」為何在此刻被推到檯面

2. Four Converging Forces: Why “Is It Worth It?” Is Surfacing Now

如前文所述，重視價值本身不是新現象。真正改變的不是消費者突然變得精打細算，而是以下四股各自累積多年的力量，在近兩年疊加到了一個更容易被感知、被公開討論的密度。它們沒有單一的起點，也不是 2026 年才出現，但共同作用的結果，讓「值不值」從背景考量變成了前台問題。

1. 購買力壓力重寫非必要消費的排序

McKinsey 在 2026 年對五個市場、4,863 名消費者的調查發現，持續的生活成本壓力使逾四分之三消費者仍採取某種節省或降級行為。在非必要品類中，人們不只更換品牌或尋找促銷，而是改變買什麼、何時買，甚至是否購買。82% 表示會延長產品使用期，69% 表示會維修而非丟棄產品。[2]

這不等於所有人都走向低價。更常見的情況是：同一個人會在低優先品類節省，卻在少數真正重要的場景中保留或提高支出。這種「選擇性升級、選擇性降級」使產品必須更清楚地回答自己的優先順位。

2. 多輪加價令新品溢價受到更直接的質疑

奢侈品不以最低價格競爭，本來就應以工藝、創意、稀缺、服務與文化意義建立溢價。問題出現在價格上升快於消費者能感受到的產品與體驗改善時。Business of Fashion 與 McKinsey 指出，2023 至 2025 年間，約 80% 的奢侈品市場增長來自價格上調，而不是銷量增長。[3] 當客戶看見同一系列多輪加價，卻沒有感受到相應的設計新意、材料升級或服務改善，「值不值」自然會成為第一道問題。

3. 二手市場讓價值由抽象變成可比較的市場訊號

二手平台展示品牌、型號、品況、供給量與價格，讓消費者在購買新品前多了一個參考點。BCG 與 Vestiaire Collective 的研究估計，全球二手時尚及奢侈品市場以約 10% 年增長，增速約為一手市場的三倍，預計至 2030 年達 3,200 億至 3,600 億美元。[4] 二手不只是「買不起新品」的替代品；它同時提供進入品牌、尋找稀有品、探索個人風格和觀察真實市場需求的途徑。

4. 資訊透明化讓比較變快，也讓品牌敘事不再自成一格

消費者現在可在購買前快速檢視新品價格歷史、二手行情、材料評價、售後經驗與同儕內容。McKinsey 的奢侈品研究發現，客戶愈來愈透過 AI、轉售平台、創作者與同儕網絡研究、比較及驗證產品。[5] 搜尋仍然是最主要的購買研究工具，AI 尚未取代搜尋；但兩者連同社群平台，都顯著降低了比較成本。[6]

As noted above, valuing a purchase is not itself new. What has changed is not that consumers suddenly became more calculating, but that the four forces below—each built up over several years—have converged to a density that is now easier to notice and easier to discuss publicly. None of them began in 2026, and none has a single origin point; together, however, they have moved “is it worth it?” from a background consideration to a foreground question.

1. Purchasing-power pressure rewrites the ranking of discretionary spending

McKinsey’s 2026 survey of 4,863 consumers across five markets found that sustained cost pressure kept more than three-quarters of consumers in some form of trade-down or saving behaviour. In discretionary categories, people are not merely switching brands or seeking promotions; they are changing what they buy, when they buy, and whether they buy at all. Eighty-two percent report using items for longer, and 69% report repairing rather than discarding them.[2]

This does not mean everyone is moving to the lowest price. More often, the same person economises in low-priority categories while preserving or increasing spending for a few meaningful occasions. This pattern of selective trading down and selective trading up makes a product’s priority in the budget more important than ever.

2. Repeated price increases invite a more direct challenge to new-product premium

Luxury does not compete on the lowest price, and it should command premium through craft, creativity, scarcity, service, and cultural meaning. The difficulty comes when prices rise faster than customers can perceive improvements in product and experience. Business of Fashion and McKinsey estimate that around 80% of luxury-market growth between 2023 and 2025 came from price increases rather than volume growth.[3] When clients see repeated increases for the same line without commensurate design renewal, material improvement, or service, “is it worth it?” becomes the first question.

3. Resale turns an abstract idea of value into comparable market signals

Resale platforms display brands, models, condition, supply, and prices, giving consumers another point of reference before buying new. BCG and Vestiaire Collective estimate the global secondhand fashion and luxury market is growing at roughly 10% annually—about three times faster than firsthand—and could reach $320–360 billion by 2030.[4] Resale is not only an alternative for those priced out of new luxury. It is also a route into brands, rare items, personal-style discovery, and real-market observation.

4. Information transparency makes comparison faster and brand storytelling less self-contained

Consumers can now quickly inspect price histories, resale levels, material reviews, service experiences, and peer content before purchase. McKinsey’s luxury research finds that clients increasingly use AI, resale platforms, creators, and peer networks to research, compare, and validate products.[5] Search remains the leading research tool and AI has not displaced it, but together with social platforms these tools have materially lowered the cost of comparison.[6]

三、資料檢驗：美、日、澳的必需支出比重均上升，但不能直接作國際排名

3. A Data Check: Essential-Cost Shares Rose in the US, Japan, and Australia—but They Are Not an International Ranking

「生活成本擠壓可選消費」需要回到支出結構驗證。以下以美國、日本及澳洲為例，比較 2016 年左右與截至 2026 年 9 月可得的最新完整年度。選取三地是因為它們是成熟、資料可得的消費市場，不是因為三者皆為進口型經濟：日本的能源和原材料進口依賴度相對高，但美國與尤其澳洲的經濟及貿易結構不同，後者更是重要的資源出口國。因此，這裡的重點是觀察日常必需項目如何佔用消費預算，而非將三地視為同質經濟體。

為提高可重現性，本文將食品、住房／住所、能源與公用事業、交通及醫療加總，並除以各官方來源的總消費支出。這是一個分析口徑，而非官方的貧窮線或生活成本指數。三個市場的資料層級不同：美國是消費單位年度平均支出；日本是二人以上世帶的每戶月均年平均；澳洲因缺乏與最新年度配對的 Household Expenditure Survey，採用含自住者設算租金的國民帳戶 household final consumption expenditure（HFCE）總量。故占比變化可支持「預算空間受擠壓」的方向性結論，卻不能用來排列三國哪一國家庭較難負擔。

The claim that living costs crowd out discretionary consumption needs a check against expenditure structure. The comparison below uses the United States, Japan, and Australia, pairing 2016 or the closest financial year with the latest complete annual data available by September 2026. These three were selected because they are mature consumer markets with accessible official data, not because they are all “import economies.” Japan has relatively high dependence on imported energy and raw materials, but the economic and trade structures of the US—and especially Australia—are different; Australia is a major resource exporter. The purpose is therefore to observe how everyday necessities occupy consumer budgets, not to treat the three economies as homogeneous.

For reproducibility, this article adds food, housing or shelter, energy and utilities, transport, and healthcare, then divides the result by each official source’s total consumption expenditure. This is an analytical construction, not an official poverty line or cost-of-living index. The underlying data layers differ: the US reports annual average consumer-unit spending; Japan reports annual averages of monthly spending for two-or-more-person households; and Australia uses national-accounts household final consumption expenditure (HFCE), including imputed owner-occupier rent, because a matched latest Household Expenditure Survey does not exist. The movements in shares support a directional conclusion of tighter budget space, but they cannot rank which country’s households are least able to cope.

四、價值不只等於保值：有限預算下的三種「值得」

4. Value Is Not Just Resale Value: Three Forms of “Worth It” Under a Limited Budget

若把所有價值都縮減為二手價值，便無法解釋為何盲盒經濟與旅遊消費仍然強勁。盲盒多數沒有穩定的轉售價值，旅遊更幾乎不能轉售；但兩者都能在購買力受壓時獲得預算。原因是消費者不是只在購買資產，而是在購買不同形式的回報。

Reducing all value to resale value cannot explain why blind-box collectibles and travel continue to attract spending. Most blind boxes have no stable resale value, while travel is essentially non-resalable. Yet both can win budget share under pressure because consumers are not only buying assets; they are buying different forms of return.

盲盒：低單價的情緒型微奢侈，而非反理性消費

盲盒的消費邏輯不是「最耐用」或「最保值」，而是以相對低的單次金額，提供即時快樂、角色認同與可被分享的社交話題。變動獎勵、完成套裝的收集目標，以及隱藏款帶來的稀缺想像，都會放大其吸引力。部分熱門款確實會產生二級市場，但多數購買者不是為了投資，而是為了期待、展示與社群互動。

因此，盲盒高消費並不推翻價值消費。它反而說明：當大額消費需要更長時間考慮時，低金額、回報即時、情緒密度高的「小確幸」更容易被保留下來。消費者對盲盒的問題不是「它五年後能值多少」，而是「這次的快樂、身份認同與交流，是否值得這個價格」。

旅遊：不可轉售，卻具有強大的時間稀缺性

旅遊的價值更不能用二手價格衡量。人們花錢購買的是與家人相處、探索文化、休息、轉換生活節奏，以及創造可被回憶與分享的片段。它還具備強烈的時間稀缺性：自己的健康、父母是否尚可同行、孩子的成長階段、朋友能否相約，都是無法儲存或日後補買的條件。

McKinsey 的 2026 年研究指出，旅遊是美國與中國奢侈品客戶獲得額外可支配收入時的首選支出項目。[5] 這不代表人們停止買奢侈品，而是奢侈品正與更難被複製的體驗競爭預算。對品牌而言，問題不應是「如何令產品取代旅遊」，而是「如何讓產品進入人生中值得紀念的體驗」。

Blind boxes: low-ticket emotional micro-luxury, not irrational consumption

The logic of blind boxes is not “most durable” or “best retained value.” At a relatively small ticket, they offer immediate joy, character affinity, and a social object that can be shared. Variable rewards, the urge to complete a set, and the imagined scarcity of hidden editions all amplify their appeal. Some sought-after items do create secondary markets, but most consumers are buying anticipation, display, and community interaction—not an investment.

Blind-box spending therefore does not disprove value consumption. It shows that when a large purchase requires more deliberation, low-cost purchases with immediate and emotionally dense returns are easier to retain. The question is not “what will this be worth in five years?” but “is this moment of joy, identity, and exchange worth the price?”

Travel: non-resalable, but highly time-scarce

Travel cannot be measured by resale price. Consumers are buying time with family, cultural discovery, restoration, a break from routine, and memories that can be recalled and shared. It also carries strong time scarcity: one’s health, a parent’s ability to travel, a child’s stage of life, and the availability of friends cannot be stored or bought back later.

McKinsey’s 2026 research finds travel is the top choice for additional discretionary spending among luxury clients in the US and China.[5] This does not mean consumers have stopped buying luxury goods. It means luxury goods are competing with experiences that are harder to replicate. The question for brands is not “how do we replace travel?” but “how do we make our product part of a life experience worth remembering?”

五、珠寶的特殊位置：少數能同時承載三種價值的品類

5. Jewellery’s Distinct Position: One of Few Categories That Can Carry All Three Forms of Value

珠寶的競爭優勢不應只建立在「保值」。一件設計成熟的 Fine Jewellery，可能同時具備資產與選擇權價值、情緒與社交價值，以及體驗與記憶價值。它有貴金屬與寶石等材料基礎；它可成為個人風格、關係與身份的表達；它亦常嵌入求婚、婚嫁、周年、出生紀念與家族傳承等人生時刻。

這也是珠寶品牌應避免把自己過度金融化的原因。金屬與寶石可以形成某種材料基礎，但並不代表每件珠寶都能原價回收或升值。未來流通性還取決於寶石規格與證書、設計辨識度、尺寸是否可調整、維修紀錄、品況、供給量與市場需求。泛化地承諾「保值」不僅難以證明，還會把工藝與情感價值錯誤地簡化為投資回報。

更可信的主張是降低購買後的不確定性。客戶需要在三年、五年甚至更久後，仍能理解和證明自己擁有的是什麼：材料從何而來、工藝如何完成、是否有完整文件、如何保養、誰可提供維修，以及作品在品牌產品架構中的位置。這種可驗證性，會同時支持正價新品信心、長期佩戴和合理的二級市場流通。

Jewellery’s competitive advantage should not rest solely on “retained value.” A well-designed fine-jewellery piece can carry all three forms of value: asset and optionality value, emotional and social value, and experience and memory value. It has a material base in precious metals and gemstones; it can express personal style, relationships, and identity; and it often sits within life moments such as engagements, weddings, anniversaries, births, and family inheritance.

This is precisely why jewellery brands should avoid over-financialising their offer. Metals and stones can provide a material floor, but this does not mean every jewel can be resold at retail price or appreciate. Future liquidity also depends on specifications and certificates, design recognition, resizability, service history, condition, supply, and market demand. A broad claim of “value retention” is both hard to substantiate and risks reducing craft and emotion to investment return.

The more credible proposition is to reduce uncertainty after purchase. Three, five, or more years later, a client should still be able to understand and verify what they own: where materials came from, how it was made, whether documentation is complete, how it should be cared for, who can service it, and where the piece sits in the brand architecture. This verifiability supports confidence in full-price newness, long-term wear, and more orderly secondary-market circulation.

六、品牌應如何回應：不是用折扣回答一切，而是建立多層價值

6. How Brands Should Respond: Build Layered Value Rather Than Answer Everything with Discounting

面對購買力壓力，最直接的反應往往是促銷。然而，在奢侈品與 Fine Jewellery 中，過度依賴折扣容易損害客戶對正價的信心，也可能令二手價格進一步受壓。更具長期價值的做法，是由產品架構、服務資料、體驗設計與客戶關係，回應不同形式的「值得」。

1. 用產品階梯回應不同購買能力

品牌應清楚區分第一件品牌珠寶或禮贈的入門款、長期佩戴且承載品牌符號的核心款、以材質或工藝提升客單價的進階款，以及具收藏性與創作實驗的限量款。這並非只按價格分類，而是讓每個價格帶都有具體理由。入門款必須有清楚品牌特徵，不應只是被縮小的高價款；核心款則要以比例、舒適度、可搭配性與長期服務建立信任。

2. 把材料、工藝與維修資料視為產品的一部分

高價購買包含信任。金屬純度、寶石規格、製作工藝、保養指引、保修條件、改尺寸與維修紀錄，應以客戶容易保存和理解的方式交付。數碼產品護照、QR 驗證和電子保固可協助，但技術不是目的；關鍵是資料是否準確、服務責任是否清晰，以及它能否在產品流轉時維持可信。

3. 由「賣一件產品」轉向「參與一段體驗」

既然旅遊與體驗正在競爭預算，珠寶品牌就應更主動地設計與人生時刻的連結。例如，求婚或周年不只是一筆交易，還可包括挑選諮詢、訂製故事、刻字、交付儀式、保養提醒與日後紀念服務。這不是把每一次銷售都包裝成活動，而是讓服務真正回應產品的情感功能。

4. 謹慎參與轉售，先把真偽與服務能力做好

品牌不需要立即自建回購平台。更務實的起點是監測二手市場資料、建立授權維修與驗證標準、與具鑑定能力的平台合作，或只針對一個核心系列進行小規模回收、翻新及寄售試點。只有當真偽鑑定、逆向物流、品況分級、定價與客戶資料治理足夠成熟後，才適合擴大轉售計畫。

When purchasing power is under pressure, promotion is often the most immediate response. In luxury and fine jewellery, however, over-reliance on discounting can undermine confidence in full price and further pressure resale values. A more durable response is to address different forms of “worth it” through product architecture, service data, experience design, and client relationships.

1. Use product tiers to serve different purchasing capacities

Brands should distinguish between entry pieces for a first purchase or gift, core pieces for long-term wear that carry brand codes, elevated pieces that increase ticket through materials or craft, and limited pieces with collecting and creative-experiment value. This is not simply price segmentation; every price point needs a distinct reason to exist. An entry piece must have recognisable brand character rather than be a shrunken version of a high-ticket piece. Core pieces should build trust through proportion, comfort, versatility, and long-term service.

2. Treat material, craft, and service data as part of the product

High-ticket purchase includes trust. Metal fineness, gemstone specifications, making process, care guidance, warranty terms, resizing, and service records should be delivered in a form clients can retain and understand. Digital product passports, QR verification, and electronic warranties can help, but technology is not the objective. The key is accurate data, clear service responsibility, and credible continuity when the product changes hands.

3. Move from selling an item to participating in an experience

Since travel and experiences compete for budget, jewellery brands should more intentionally link themselves to life moments. An engagement or anniversary is not only a transaction; it can include selection guidance, custom storytelling, engraving, a delivery ritual, care reminders, and future commemorative service. This is not about turning every sale into an event. It is about serving the product’s real emotional function.

4. Participate in resale carefully; build authentication and service capability first

Brands do not need to launch buyback platforms immediately. More pragmatic starting points include monitoring resale data, setting authorised-service and verification standards, collaborating with capable authenticators, or piloting collection, refurbishment, and consignment for one core line. Resale should only scale after authentication, reverse logistics, condition grading, pricing, and customer-data governance are sufficiently mature.

七、未來 2–3 年：價值議題不會退場，但會由「性價比」升級為「價值組合」

7. The Next Two to Three Years: The Debate Will Persist but Evolve from “Value for Money” to a “Value Portfolio”

未來兩至三年，價值消費不太可能像短期 hashtag 一樣退場，因為它的動力並非單一美學風格，而是購買力、資訊透明度、二手市場流動性與技術工具的共同改變。全球二手服裝市場預計於 2025 至 2030 年以約 9% 年複合增長；美國線上轉售市場則預計同期以約 10% 年複合增長，快於傳統服裝零售。[7] Bain 於 2026 年 6 月的最新展望，已將個人奢侈品市場 2026 年基準情境下修至約 2% 至 4% 的溫和增長（約 3,650 至 3,730 億歐元），較 2025 年底發布的 3% 至 5% 預測略為保守，反映的仍是溫和復甦，而非重返以不斷加價為主的高速擴張。[8]

但討論會演進。下一步不會只是「便宜還是昂貴」，而是消費者如何在三種價值之間配置預算：保留有選擇權的耐用品、購買能帶來即時情緒與社交回報的小額商品，以及投資不可取代的體驗與記憶。品牌面對的不是單一競爭者，而是客戶的整體價值組合。

Over the next two to three years, value consumption is unlikely to disappear like a short-lived hashtag because its drivers are not a single aesthetic trend. They are the combined effects of purchasing power, information transparency, resale liquidity, and technology. The global secondhand apparel market is projected to grow at about 9% CAGR from 2025 to 2030, while US online resale is projected to grow around 10% annually over the same period—faster than traditional apparel retail.[7] Bain’s June 2026 update revised its 2026 base-case outlook for personal luxury goods down to about 2–4% growth (roughly €365–373 billion), slightly more conservative than the 3–5% forecast issued at the end of 2025—still a moderate recovery rather than a return to an era of high growth driven mainly by continuous price elevation.[8]

The conversation will evolve, however. The next question will not simply be “cheap or expensive.” It will be how consumers allocate their budget across three forms of value: durable goods that preserve optionality, low-ticket products that provide immediate emotional and social return, and non-substitutable experiences and memories. Brands are not competing with one rival; they are competing with a client’s entire value portfolio.

結論：品牌的定價權，取決於能否在有限預算中被優先選擇

Conclusion: Pricing Power Depends on Being Chosen First Within a Finite Budget

2026 年的消費者不是突然變得只看價格，也不是不再願意為奢侈品付費。UNIQLO 的例子已證明，簡約、功能、品質與合理價格本來就是長期產品哲學。今天的改變在於，購買力壓力令更多人必須排序支出；奢侈品的多輪加價令溢價更受檢驗；二手、搜尋、社群和 AI 則讓比較更快、更公開。

二手價值是這場討論的重要部分，但不是全部。盲盒提醒品牌，即時的情緒與社交回報能夠吸收預算；旅遊提醒品牌，難以複製的體驗與時間稀缺性同樣能擊敗物質商品。消費者不會只為可轉售的東西付錢。他們會淘汰那些既沒有長期使用或選擇權、沒有情緒與社交意義、也沒有體驗與記憶價值的商品。

對珠寶品牌而言，這既是壓力也是機會。珠寶是少數可能同時承載三種價值的品類：它能長期佩戴、維修與傳承；它能表達關係與身份；它也能成為人生重要時刻的一部分。品牌不需承諾每件作品都升值，也不應以折扣回答所有購買力問題。更重要的是建立清晰的產品階梯、可信的品質與資料、持續的售後服務，以及能讓作品進入客戶人生故事的體驗。

奢侈品下一階段的定價權，不是來自品牌單方面標出更高價格，而是來自品牌能否在客戶有限的預算中，持續成為最值得被優先選擇的一項。

Consumers in 2026 have not suddenly become price-only shoppers, nor have they stopped paying for luxury. UNIQLO has long demonstrated that simplicity, function, quality, and reasonable pricing can be a durable product philosophy. What has changed is that purchasing-power pressure makes more people rank their spending; repeated luxury price rises subject premium to more scrutiny; and resale, search, social content, and AI make comparison faster and more public.

Resale value is an important part of this discussion, but not the whole of it. Blind boxes remind brands that immediate emotional and social return can command budget. Travel reminds brands that irreplaceable experiences and time scarcity can also beat material goods. Consumers will not pay only for what can be resold. They will increasingly reject products that offer neither long-term use or optionality, emotional and social meaning, nor experience and memory value.

For jewellery brands, this is both pressure and opportunity. Jewellery is among the few categories able to carry all three values: it can be worn, repaired, and passed on; it can express relationships and identity; and it can become part of important life moments. Brands do not need to promise every piece will appreciate, nor should they answer every purchasing-power challenge with discounting. They need clear product tiers, credible quality and data, sustained aftercare, and experiences that place the work within a client’s life story.

Luxury’s next source of pricing power will not be a brand’s unilateral ability to set a higher price. It will be its ability to remain the option clients choose first within a finite budget.

AI 減速倡議不等於停止 AI。真正的問題是：當各國與企業都擔心對手先取得優勢時，誰願意先放慢？本文以 Simunovic 等人的先發制人實驗，分析恐懼、不確定性如何形成 AI 競賽僵局，並拆解倡議的受益者、市場反應，以及未來安全監管與美中競爭可能如何並行發展。

AI pacing does not mean stopping AI. The harder question is: when every country and company fears that a rival may gain the advantage first, who is willing to slow down first? This article uses Simunovic et al.'s preemptive-strike experiment to explain how fear and uncertainty sustain the AI race, then examines who benefits, how markets reacted, and why safety regulation and US-China competition may advance in parallel.
