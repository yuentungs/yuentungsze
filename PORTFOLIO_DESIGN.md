# Luxury Product Portfolio — Experience Design

## Purpose

Create a bilingual, recruiter-ready product strategy portfolio for a senior jewellery product manager. The page should feel like an editorial casebook rather than a student assignment: sparse luxury cues, clear analytical frameworks, cited market inputs and an interactive margin model.

## Information Architecture

| Section | Reader question | Primary evidence / interaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | What does this portfolio prove? | Three case identifiers, role statement, direct links to cases. |
| Project 01 — Assortment | How do two iconic maisons build demand across categories and price tiers? | Architecture ladder, brand comparison, customer/occasion mapping and strategic takeaway. |
| Project 02 — SEA market | Which market should be prioritized? | 2025 World Bank macro table, country scorecard, qualitative wealth/tourism/gold lenses and a clear decision. |
| Project 03 — Pricing | Can the candidate translate price into SKU-level contribution? | Interactive necklace P&L and live price/volume scenario comparison. |
| Methodology | How should the reader interpret this evidence? | Sources, definitions, country-scoring caveat and model assumptions. |

## Visual System

> **Style:** Editorial dark atelier — black ink, warm ivory, restrained champagne gold, deep emerald accents, fine-grid texture and a serif/sans-serif pairing.

| Token | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `#10100f` | Primary dark canvas and high-contrast story sections. |
| `#f5f0e8` | Warm paper surfaces and body text contrast. |
| `#c8a36b` | Champagne-gold emphasis, dividers and numerical highlights. |
| `#1e4b43` | Emerald evidence and interactive accent. |
| Cormorant Garamond + Manrope | Luxury editorial hierarchy paired with analytical readability. |

## Content Principles

The public page shall distinguish **source-backed observations** from **portfolio assumptions**. Brand price bands use US online catalogue snapshots and must not be treated as global retail parity. The pricing simulator uses illustrative economics and labels fixed versus retail-linked cost assumptions. The market recommendation prioritizes a city-first Singapore launch for prestige and clienteling, not as investment advice.

## Editing Model

The portfolio remains a plain static page. Copy and qualitative scorecards live in `portfolio.html`; macro inputs are in `assets/data/sea_market_indicators.json`, generated by `scripts/fetch_market_indicators.py`. This makes future edits possible through the GitHub web editor without installing software.
