中國汽車市場的十年變革：燃油車與新能源汽車的興衰與未來 (2014-2030)China's Automotive Market Transformation: The Rise and Fall of ICE vs. NEV (2014-2030)

發佈日期：2026年6月15日 | 分類：市場趨勢分析Published: June 15, 2026 | Category: Market Trend Analysis

引言：Introduction: 過去十年，中國汽車市場經歷了一場由新能源汽車（NEV）引領的顛覆性變革。本報告深入分析 2014 至 2024 年間的市場演變，並對 2027 至 2030 年的未來趨勢進行預測。 Over the past decade, China's automotive market has undergone a transformative revolution led by new energy vehicles (NEV). This report provides an in-depth analysis of market evolution from 2014 to 2024 and forecasts trends through 2030.

一、 市場佔有率對比：新能源汽車的中國速度1. Market Share Comparison: China's NEV Speed

中國新能源汽車市場的崛起速度令人驚嘆。從 2014 年的萌芽期到 2024 年的近半壁江山，滲透率呈現指數級增長。The rise of China's NEV market is astounding. From nascent beginnings in 2014 to nearly half the market by 2024, penetration rates show exponential growth.

二、 本土龍頭企業2. Local Industry Leaders

比亞迪（BYD）作為領軍者，以及吉利、長安等傳統車企的成功轉型，加上「蔚小理」等造車新勢力的崛起，構成了獨特風景。BYD as market leader, alongside successful transformations by traditional automakers like Geely and Changan, plus the rise of new EV makers like Nio, XPeng, and Li Auto, create a unique competitive landscape.

三、 未來展望 (2027-2030)3. Future Outlook (2027-2030)

  • 固態電池量產：寧德時代與比亞迪計劃於 2027 年實現小規模量產，徹底解決里程焦慮。Solid-State Battery Production: CATL and BYD plan small-scale production by 2027, solving range anxiety.
  • 自動駕駛商業化：L3 級別自動駕駛將逐步普及，Robotaxi 預計在 2030 年實現大規模商業化。Autonomous Driving Commercialization: L3 autonomous driving will gradually proliferate, with Robotaxi expected to scale by 2030.
  • 品牌出海：中國新能源車佔世界份額已達 69%，全球化佈局將成為企業增長的新引擎。Global Expansion: Chinese NEVs already account for 69% of global market share, making international expansion a key growth engine.

作者觀點：Author's Perspective: 這場變革不僅是技術的更迭，更是商業模式與能源結構的重塑。作為產品經理，我們必須具備跨行業的數據洞察力，才能在波動的市場中精準定位。This transformation is not merely technological but a reshaping of business models and energy structures. As product managers, we must possess cross-industry data insights to position accurately in volatile markets.
